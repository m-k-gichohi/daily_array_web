Pinterest Integration

Our internal application connects to Pinterest using Pinterest's official API. The application is used only by our organization to create, schedule, and publish Pins for products featured on The Daily Array website.

The Pinterest integration may be used to:

  • Create Pins

We only request permissions necessary for publishing and managing our own Pinterest content. We do not sell, rent, or share Pinterest account data with third parties.
